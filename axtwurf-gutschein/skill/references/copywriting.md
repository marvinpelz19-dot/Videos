# Copywriting

The film speaks in short, declarative, product-true lines. The words are the
narration: there is no voiceover.

## Voice

- Plain verbs, concrete nouns, present tense. No hype adjectives
  ("revolutionary", "seamless", "next-level").
- 2-7 words per line, at most two lines on screen, at most three for a list
  line such as "Install one. / Run it end to end. / Keep what it makes."
- One payoff per sentence, colored with `[accent]`. The payoff is the
  benefit, not the product name.
- Use the product's own vocabulary and example tasks from its site. If the
  site says "Bots", the film says "Bots"; if it says "Skills", say "Skills".
- English by default, even for non-English requests, unless the user asks for
  another language (then check the font covers it).

## Patterns that carry the style

| Beat | Pattern | Example |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Cold open | `@mark {Version}` -> `@mark {Product} {Version}` | "@mark Northwind 2.0" |
| Introducing | `@mark Introducing {Product}` | |
| Hero word | `{Verb}` (470 px) -> `{Verb} ... [{promise}]` | "Hand" -> "Hand off the [busywork]"; "Plan" -> "Plan less, [ship more]"; "Ask" -> "Ask once, get [the whole report]" |
| Divider | `New {Chapter}` (one noun each; reuse one list) | New Engine / New Workspace / New Mobile |
| Dock caption | noun list that collapses into a claim | "Slides. Web. Images. Video." -> "One library for [all of it.]" |
| Feature card | `{Verb} {object}` | "Write reports", "Plan launches", "Answer tickets" |
| Statement | benefit in the user's words | "The draft is [ready by morning.]" |
| Plate title | 2-4 words over the landscape | "Runs on your schedule", "Private by default" |
| Trigger -> action | "When/Every ..." -> "Do ..." (real use cases) | "Every Friday at 5 PM." -> "Summarize the week for the team." |
| Photo headline | two-beat line, left aligned | "Done / [before you're back.]"; "In your / [pocket.]" |
| Reveal | "One More Thing" -> `New {Thing}` -> pill "Meet {Thing}!" | "Meet Northwind Go!" |
| Close 1 | `{Product} gives you {benefit}` with `insert` on the key word | "Northwind gives you your ‸hours back" |
| Close 2 | a short line that hands the choice to the viewer, `circle` on "You" | "You choose" -> "You choose what ships" |

## Truth rules

- Numbers only when they are on the product's site (counts, prices,
  benchmarks) and still current; label them as they appear there.
- UI typing must be a request the product really handles (take it from the
  site's examples).
- Do not name customers, people or partners unless the site does and the
  user wants them in the film.
- If a feature is "coming soon" on the site, say so or leave it out.
